I am excited to share the first public milestone of AutoTriage, a vehicle-damage assessment product I have been building from research into a usable application.

AutoTriage lets a driver capture or upload a vehicle image and receive:

- damage-type screening
- severity and urgency guidance
- indicative repair-cost ranges in AED
- a clear recommendation for when professional review is needed
- an optional feedback loop for improving future model versions

The system is now running as a private cloud API with a persistent data layer, health and readiness checks, protected admin workflows, consent-based feedback, and a mobile client prepared for iOS and Android.

The first release intentionally uses cautious language: this is a screening aid, not a mechanic, insurance assessment, repair quotation, or guarantee that a vehicle is safe to drive.

The next phase is focused on real-world validation, verified Dubai repair partners, and improving the detector with carefully reviewed user feedback.

This project sits at the intersection of computer vision, product engineering, and responsible AI: useful enough to act on, transparent about uncertainty, and designed around human review where it matters.
